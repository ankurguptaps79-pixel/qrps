About us

Pradeep Sales is an authorized automotive spare parts distributor based in Prayagraj, specialising in three-wheeler aftermarket components.

Founded by Late Mr. Suresh Chandra Gupta and carried forward by the next generation, we pair deep product knowledge with dependable supply, so every mechanic and retailer in our network finds the right part, on time.
